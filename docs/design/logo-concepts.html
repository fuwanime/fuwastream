FuwaStream logo concepts

Three directions for the launcher icon, each tested in circle, squircle and rounded-square masks, at 48 px and 24 px, and as an Android 13+ themed (monochrome) icon. Colors are the app's Yozakura palette; the wordmark uses Plus Jakarta Sans, already bundled in the app (SIL Open Font License).

Positioning: For Indonesian anime and manga fans, FuwaStream is the free app that keeps watching and reading in one soft, ad-free place.

Now: petal-play

current

Reads as an arrowhead more than a sakura petal; the sharp tip fights "fuwa" (soft); nothing says manga; the themed icon is a bare arrow.

recommended

A soft cloud (fuwa = fluffy) with a rounded play cut out of its belly and a small book-spine notch at the base: watching and reading inside one soft shape. A pink dot keeps the sakura accent.

fuwastream
Why
  • The only direction that draws the word "fuwa" itself.
  • The cut-out play survives one-colour use, so the themed icon still says "video".
Watch out
  • A cloud alone can read as weather or backup; the play cut-out has to stay big.
  • The spine notch disappears below 32 px (fine: it is a detail, not the idea).

B · Origami page-play

evolution

Keeps today's two-tone sakura idea but splits it into a pink manga page and a lavender play wedge with a clean seam. Every corner is rounded, so the needle tip is gone.

fuwastream
Why
  • The clearest "manga + anime" read: a page next to a play.
  • Closest to the current icon, so existing users still recognise it.
Watch out
  • Less "fuwa": it is soft but not fluffy.
  • The 4dp seam is about 2 px at 48 px; any thinner and the two shapes merge.

C · Koma portal

bold

A soft squircle frame that is both a manga panel (koma) and a screen, with a pink play inside and a bookmark ribbon on top.

fuwastream
Why
  • Strongest at 24 px: a solid frame and one mark.
  • Fills every launcher mask evenly.
Watch out
  • Framed-play icons are common (YouTube-like), so it is the least distinctive.
  • The bookmark is lost in the themed icon.
